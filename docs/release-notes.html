WHAT’S NEW

Release notes

What changed in each version, newest first.

What changed in each version of Church Service Timer, newest first, in short. Update every phone, tablet and PC your team uses to the same version: from 0.28, program files, backups and sent programs can't be opened by 0.27 or earlier.

0.39.0 · 24 November 2026

New: Screens

  • One page for every screen. Screens is a button on Live (beside Change display and Slides, or an icon in the top bar in a desktop layout), a place in the side bar or the tabs along the top on a tablet or PC, and Screens in Commands (Ctrl+K). The Screens buttons in Slides and in Settings open the same page. See Choose the right display.
  • Groups. Screens are sorted into groups you name, and one tap on a group's picture tile sets what all its screens show: Timer only, Timer and segment name, Timer and up next, Up next only, Slides, or the new Slides and timer. Move a screen to another group at any time, even mid-service. Groups can be added, renamed and deleted. Nothing asks "are you sure". A phone or tablet paired for the first time joins the group marked default for new screens (Stage to begin with); a browser joins the group of the web link it was opened from, or the group set for New web screens.
  • Your screens keep showing what they showed. The update makes the groups from your settings: Church Auditorium (this computer's second screen, with full screen and the stage simulator, and web screens) and Stage (paired phones and tablets). Screens that were set differently get a group of their own, and screens ticked under the old Slides Send to are in a group set to Slides. A paired screen's old Screen group for quick messages is now called Message group.
  • Slides and timer is a confidence monitor: the words of the slide large on the left, the countdown on the right (amber in the warning time, red in overtime) with the next slide's name and first line under it. A paired screen on an older version shows the countdown instead.
  • Scenes save what every group shows, and one tap sets them all again. A program segment can name a scene, and every group changes by itself when that segment starts. Groups and scenes travel in backups when you have scenes; that backup needs 0.39.0 or later.
  • Shows cues, for each group. On, a group's screens show cues, warnings and quick messages, also over a slide (a band across the bottom or top, or in the timer panel of Slides and timer). Off, they never do. Church Auditorium starts with Shows cues off, so WRAP UP and other cues no longer appear on the second screen and web screens until you switch it on. Stage starts on, and new groups start off.
  • The LED sign has a card on Screens ("Shows the countdown and cues. Never slides."), and Live shows a small picture of what was last sent to it above the stage preview. It is never in a group.
  • Screens on a phone stays a button on Live. The bottom bar keeps five places.

Navigation and windows

  • A new order: Live, Slides, Screens, Programs, Reports, Settings. What you use during a service comes first. On a phone: Live, Slides, Programs, Reports, Settings.
  • Pages use the whole window. Screens fills a wide window with as many columns of groups as fit. Screens & devices fills its panes the same way. Settings runs across a wide window, in three columns when there is room.
  • Tapping a notification opens the app on Live. The countdown warning on a phone, the lock-screen card for a running service and the Windows warning toast bring the app to the front on Live, with the service running as it was. The app is not restarted.

Slides

  • Search the Bible, and a Ready tray. The box at the top of the Library searches your songs and the whole King James Bible as you type: a reference, or any words from a verse in any order. Enter puts a passage in Ready, and Send shows it like any slide. + next verse, Whole chapter and Save to Library are in Ready. / or Ctrl+F goes to the box, and Search the Bible is in Commands. See Show words with Slides.
  • Bible references are forgiving. Misspelt books are put right, words work (John chapter three verse sixteen), and a short name that could be several books shows Did you mean: with a button for each.
  • A tidier Slides page. Previous, Next, Clear and Black sit in one row. Under the preview are three rows, Screens, Background and Look; Background and Look start closed, and the app remembers which you left open. The Library has tabs: Songs, Bible verses, Announcements and All, and the Add buttons are one row, + Song, + Bible verses, + Announcement.
  • Slide cards line up at an even height, and repeat marks such as _(x2)_ show as (x2) on every screen.
  • Typing in the Slides editors no longer stops after you clear a box and type the same words again.

Finishes

  • Real materials, with no slowdown. Every texture is now a photograph of the real thing, drawn once and kept, so a finish is no slower than Flat. Edges are drawn to match: wood inlay, polished marble, moulded plastic, bevelled steel with screws, stitched Denim and Leather.
  • Every page and every popup. Programs, Reports, the program editor, Settings, Screens & devices, dialogs, menus, pickers and Commands are made of the finish. Small cards are made of the material too, and nothing runs over a panel's edge.
  • Full by default. A finish shows at Full until you choose otherwise, except Walnut, Oak and Carbon fibre, which start at Subtle. A Subtle saved by an older version counts as not chosen, once. The tiles have a new order. See Make the workspace yours.

Other changes

  • Services end on the clock. When the service ends starts on Clock, including the Default end screen. Programs you already have, and the default, move to the clock once if they still have the plain Hallelujah that came with the app; an end screen you changed is kept. See Finish and hand over cleanly.
  • Keep screen awake is one row in Settings that opens its three choices.
  • A new look for the Windows installer. The setup window is dark and matches the app. What is installed, and how updates and removal work, are unchanged.

0.38.0 · 23 November 2026

New: Slides

  • Song words, Bible verses and announcements on the screens. A new Slides page, after Programs in the navigation, and a Slides button on Live under the display preview, put words on the second screen, web screens and stage screens. The app now has five places: Live, Programs, Slides, Reports and Settings. See Show words with Slides.
  • A Library on the left keeps every song, set of Bible verses and announcement you save, for every service, most recently used first. Search the library finds a title or any words in it. Edit or delete (it asks first) from the ⋮ beside each one.
  • Add a song by typing or pasting the words, or with Open a text file. A blank line starts a new slide, and a line such as Verse 1, Chorus, Bridge, Refrain, Pre-chorus, Intro, Outro, Tag or Ending names the slide and is not shown. Lines on a slide keeps the words as typed or splits them into slides of 2 or 4 lines, and the slides it makes show beside the words as you type.
  • Add Bible verses by typing a reference such as John 3:16, John 3:16-18, Psalm 23 or 1 Cor 13:4-7 and choosing Find. The King James Version comes with the app, and each slide ends with the reference in gold ("John 3:16 · KJV"). Other translations, such as the NLT, are under copyright: Paste your own takes the words from any translation your church may show, with its own reference and translation name.
  • Add an announcement: a title, the words, and a date, time or place line.
  • Click or tap a slide to show it. The slide on the screens is ringed in red and the next one is dashed. Previous, Next, Clear slide and Black are in On the screens now, and along the bottom on a phone or tablet. On a computer, Right arrow or Space is next, Left arrow back, Esc clears the slide and B turns the screens black. These keys work on the Slides page only: on Live, Space and the arrows pause and move to the next segment as before.
  • Send to: Second screen, Web screens and Stage screens. Stage screens start unticked, so a speaker's countdown is never covered by accident. The LED sign never shows slides. Clear slide gives every screen back what it showed before: the countdown, the clock, a cue or the end screen. A quick message to the stage still shows over a slide.
  • The look: twelve drawn backgrounds, 23 public-domain and CC0 photos under Nature and People, or Your picture; Dim (70% to begin with, up to 90%); text size S, M or L; Centre or Left; Motion Still or Gentle; and Countdown in the corner, which turns into a red chip in overtime. Your picture shows on the second screen, full screen and web screens; phones and tablets acting as stage screens show Midnight in its place for now.
  • Fits the rest of the app. The Slides page takes the Finish you chose. While a slide or Black is on a screen, the device stays awake under While a service is running, and lets go when you clear it. Slides can be shown with no service running, and ending a service keeps everything.
  • Backups carry the Library and the look. A backup that holds songs needs 0.38.0 or later to open; one without is unchanged. Your picture stays on the device. A phone, tablet or web screen on an earlier version keeps showing the countdown.

0.37.0 · 23 November 2026

On stage screens

  • A flip clock whenever a stage screen shows the time of day. After Show clock, when a service or program ends on the clock (no end screen, a Quick timer included), before a scheduled service, and under a banner cue, stage screens show hours and minutes on cards that fold over, with the weekday and the date under them. 12 hour has AM and PM tiles with the current one lit; 24 hour has two digits and no tiles. It follows the theme, and with Reduced motion the digits change without folding. The LED sign is unchanged. See Choose the right display.
  • Four layouts, with a new one: Up next only. Timer only, Timer and segment name (a NOW band across the top), Timer and up next (an UP NEXT band along the bottom) and Up next only, which shows just the next item and no time. On the last segment, in a Quick timer or with no program it shows the end screen, or the clock. A line along the bottom edge shows how much of the segment has gone; the digits and the line turn amber in the warning period; a paused countdown is dimmed and says PAUSED.
  • Picture tiles to choose the layout. On the second screen, each paired stage screen, web screens, full screen on a phone and the Stage simulator, the choice is now four picture tiles. See Pair a phone or tablet and Second screen and full screen.
  • Stage screens follow the theme. They were always white on black. Light themes work too. Cues, messages and Blank keep their own backgrounds.
  • Overtime is hard to miss. The whole stage screen turns solid red, with large dark digits counting up and OVER TIME. A new setting, Overtime alert in Settings › Display settings, sets how strongly: Gentle, Strong (the default) or Strongest, which pulses slowly. It is kept on this device, not in backups.
  • An older paired screen shows the plain clock, and the segment name instead of Up next only. Web screens always match the app.

On every device

  • Finish: choose what the app is made of. Settings › Appearance › Theme has a new Finish section with picture tiles: Flat (the default), Glass, Bridge console, Brick, Cashmere, Denim, Plastic, Walnut, Oak, Leather, Marble and Carbon fibre, with Subtle (the default) or Full strength. High contrast switches it off, and it never applies to stage screens, the second screen, web screens or the LED sign. Paper & Ink and Paper & Ink (blue) always come with their own Papery finish, which is not a tile. See Make the workspace yours.
  • The screen stays on only while a service is running. Keep screen awake in Settings now has three choices: While a service is running (the new default), Always (the old behaviour) and Never. A stage screen always stays on. If you had switched the old setting off, it stays Never; everyone else moves to the new default. An idle phone can now sleep on Live, in Settings, Programs and Reports.
  • Send message no longer insists on text that fits the LED sign when no sign is in use. The width check now applies only when the display in use is an LED sign, or the Simulator standing in for one. With stage screens, the second screen or the Stage simulator, any width can be sent, and a short note says if the text would be too wide for a sign. The same applies to the stage cue editor, the end screen and zero screen editors, warning cues, the program editor and importing a program. See Send rich Quick messages.
  • Nothing here changes programs or backups. Earlier versions open the same files.

0.36.0 · 22 November 2026

Full screen

  • Full screen follows both landscapes on Android. It turns between the two landscapes with the phone, even when auto-rotate is off. Leaving full screen puts the screen back as it was.
  • On a phone or tablet, End service leaves full screen. From the tray, from Live or from a paired device, full screen closes and the screen turns back. A PC is unchanged: full screen stays on the end screen, or the clock, until you leave it. This corrects the 0.35.0 note below, which said full screen stays open however the service ends, on any device.
  • A tip the first time. A short overlay shows the three double-tap zones and how to open the control tray. It goes with a tap, any key or after about 8 seconds. Show gestures in the tray brings it back. See Second screen and full screen.

On every device

  • "Updated to 0.36.0". The first time the app opens on a new version, Live shows a notice while standing by, with See what's new and Dismiss. It shows once, and never during a service. See Get started on Android.
  • Send message, the end screen and the zero screen follow the simulator. They now have the LED sign | Stage screen switch the cue editor has, and show the settings for the display in use. What is sent is unchanged.
  • Check for updates ignores test versions. Only real releases are offered.

On a PC

  • The same text on every PC. The app's text is drawn in Roboto, as on Android. Letters look a little different, and labels fit as they do on a phone.
  • Ctrl+K and the other Ctrl keys keep working. They could work once and then only now and then. This is fixed.
  • ? Keys and Ctrl K Commands always show at the end of the line of keys, even in a narrow window.
  • Classic balances its columns. In a narrow window it no longer leaves a large gap under the timer card, and the controls stay in view from 600 wide up.

0.35.0 · 21 November 2026

Full screen

  • Gestures in full screen. Double tap or double-click the right third of the screen for the next segment, the middle to pause or resume, and the left third to undo the last segment change. Each asks first, in the same small card as the keys and buttons. See Second screen and full screen.
  • A control tray in full screen. A single tap or click, a swipe up from the bottom edge, or the mouse at the bottom of the screen brings up a small tray: Pause or Resume, Next, −2 min, +2 min, Undo, Cues, Clock, Blank, End service and Exit. Every timer action in it still asks first. It hides after 6 seconds; Esc, Back or a swipe down puts it away. Nothing of it is sent to any stage screen.
  • Full screen stays on the end screen. On a PC, however the service ends, full screen stays open (a phone or tablet leaves it from 0.36.0) and shows the end screen, or the clock. The timer buttons grey out, while Cues, Clock, Blank and Exit keep working. It closes when the next service is set up.
  • Lock controls blocks all of it. With the controls locked, the gestures and the tray do nothing, and a tap still shows Hold to unlock.

On a PC

  • A clearer line in Ready. With a pre-service start time set, the line now reads "Timing has not started. The display shows the pre-service countdown until you start the first segment." in every layout.
  • Ready shows the timer you will have. A service that is loaded but not started now shows each desktop layout's own timer, at the size and in the place it has once running, with the first segment's full time and not counting. The Ready form sits where the timer controls go. Keys shows its own strip in Ready. See Run the service from a PC.
  • The ring fills the middle pane at every window size in Three panes and The dial, not only from 1920 × 1080, and Mission control's dial grows into spare height. A laptop, or a PC with display scaling, no longer has empty space above and below the ring.
  • The line of keys shows on laptop screens too. Three panes and The dial now list the keys along the bottom wherever there is room, not only in a window 800 high: every common screen from 1280 × 720 up, 1366 × 768 and 1270 × 790 included. Only a window 600 high leaves it out.
  • Classic fits a laptop window. In a window 1200 or more wide and less than 1000 high, Classic puts the controls beside the ring, so they are in view without scrolling.
  • Classic looks right in a window under 1200 wide, which uses Classic whatever the choice: a 1366 × 768 screen at 125% scaling, or a 1024 × 768 screen. The ring is larger, under the segment's name or beside it, and the card fills the height. Custom stays on one line, the preview heading shortens to PREVIEW where it must, and cue names no longer break in the middle of a word.

On every device

  • No end screen: show the clock. A program can end on the clock. In the Program editor the end screen card is now When the service ends, with End screen or Clock. Settings › Display settings › Default end screen has the same choice for new programs and Quick timers. With Clock, every display shows the clock when the service ends, Live says Showing the clock, and the End service question says so. See Finish and hand over cleanly.
  • Check for updates automatically. A new switch in Settings › About, off to begin with. When on, the app checks at most once a day and never during a service, and a newer version shows a notice on Live with See update and Not now. Nothing is downloaded or installed without asking. See Get started on Android.
  • The time on screens holds still, before the service too. The countdown to a scheduled start, and the clock shown before it, no longer shift or change size each second on stage screens, the second screen, full screen, web screens and the Stage simulator. On a PC these screens, and the large time in the mini timer, now use the app's own font.
  • Release notes say how to install on Windows. The lines under each release's notes now say which file is for Android and which for Windows, what to do if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", and that a PC on 0.34.0 or later can use Download and install instead.

Keep every device on the same version

  • A backup or program file in which a program, or the default, is set to end on the Clock needs 0.35 or later to open. A paired phone or tablet on an earlier version still shows the clock.

0.34.0 · 21 November 2026

Before and during a service

  • Check this service. One button runs a whole program through on the Simulator in about 18 seconds and lists every warning that fires, and any that cannot. Nothing reaches the stage and no report is saved. See Build a reusable program.
  • Up next on stage screens. A screen set to Timer and up next says who is next and for how long, such as "Next: Response · Worship team · 6 min 30 s". See Pair a phone or tablet.
  • Full screen on the device running the service. When your own screen is the one people watch, Live can show the stage countdown on it, with F11 on a PC. A phone or tablet turns to landscape and turns back when you leave. Every timer action still asks first, in a small card over the stage. See Second screen and full screen.
  • No warning the moment a segment starts. A warning fires only when the countdown comes down to its time, so a 5-minute segment no longer begins with 5 MIN LEFT. TIME'S UP at 0:00 still fires at zero.
  • Choose what the Simulator simulates: an LED sign or a stage screen. The chip reads LED simulator or Stage simulator. See Choose the right display.

Cues and warnings

  • New cues and warnings to start with: seven cues on keys 1 to 7 (WRAP UP, TIME'S UP, SPEAK UP, SLOW DOWN, MIC OFF?, FINAL SONG and SEE ME) and four warnings (5 MIN LEFT, 2 MIN LEFT, WRAP UP and TIME'S UP). Cues and warnings you changed are left alone. See Prepared cues and automatic warnings.
  • Cues on screens. A cue can have a colour (White, Amber, Red, Green or your theme's colour), sit in a banner at the top or bottom while the countdown stays in view, wrap onto up to 3 lines, and scroll at its own Speed on screens. A coloured cue that fills the screen sits on warm charcoal with a band of its colour. The LED sign is not changed by any of it.
  • Five new effects: Flash then hold, Pulse, Slide in, Countdown bar and Screen flash. Flash and Pulse show long text a few words at a time; Slide in, Countdown bar and Screen flash scroll on the LED sign when the text is too wide for it.
  • A clearer cue editor. The LED sign | Stage screen switch over the preview also chooses which settings are shown, and the panes beside Stage cues and Warnings & alerts show what your display will show.
  • A clearer warning editor, set out as When, What it shows, Where, How long and On or off: two columns on a PC, the whole screen on a phone. "Operator + phone notification" is now Operator only.
  • Smoother scrolling cues on a second monitor, web screens and the simulators.

On a PC and a tablet

  • Install an update from the app. On Windows, Check for updates offers Download and install. The installer is checked, and started only after you confirm. The Windows installer is now published beside the Android app. See Get started on Windows.
  • Tablet layout. A 10-inch or larger tablet in landscape gets the wide layouts, under Settings › Tablet layout. Phones no longer show a desktop layout setting.
  • Mission control is the layout a new install starts in. If you already use the app, your layout stays as it is.
  • Live makes better use of a large screen in every desktop layout except Keys.
  • See the second screen on Live. While the stage shows on a projector or second monitor, the Display preview holds a small live copy of it.
  • The mini timer button is at the top of every page, and the mini timer opens with no service running, showing the time of day. Click the app's name, the service's name or your church's name on any tab to go back to Live.
  • More keys listed. The line of keys includes J Jump to segment, and Three panes and The dial list the keys too.
  • Clicker and foot pedal works on a PC. Switched off, Space, B, Page Down and → do nothing, from a clicker or the keyboard. See Keyboard, clicker and Commands.
  • A calendar for the reports date filter on a PC, with ready-made ranges and a dot on each day that has a report. See Read, export and print reports.
  • Dates and times the way the app writes them: "Fri 20 Nov 2026", 20/11/2026, a 24-hour clock, and weeks that start on Monday.
  • Fixed: the buttons under the Keys rundown in a 1200 × 600 window, and New program jumping into place on Programs.

Keep every device on the same version

  • A backup or program file with any of the new cue settings (a colour, a banner, wrapped text, its own screen speed, a new effect, long Flash or Pulse text, or more than six cues) needs 0.34 or later to open.
  • A paired phone or tablet on an earlier version shows every cue in white and full screen, with the new effects as steady text.
